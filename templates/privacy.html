Affinia is a Telegram Mini App that lets two adults record that they have both agreed to an intimate connection, keep a trust network of friends, and support the project with Telegram Stars. This policy explains what data we store, why, for how long, and how you can access or delete it.

1. Who is responsible

The app is operated by the Affinia project team (the "controller"). Affinia is an early-stage product; the legal entity, registered address and data-protection contact will be published here once incorporated.

3. What a consent record is and is not

A consent record shows that two Telegram accounts each tapped "accept" at a given time, and whether one of them later revoked it. It is stored in a regular database with timestamps. It is not end-to-end encrypted, not anchored in a blockchain, not a digital signature in the legal sense, and we make no claim about how courts in any country will treat it. Consent to intimacy can be withdrawn at any moment, by words or actions, regardless of what this app shows.

4. Who can see your data

  • People you are linked with see your Telegram name and avatar and the records you share with them.
  • Nobody else can look you up: the app has no search, directory or public profile.
  • We do not sell or share data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • The project operator receives a notification when a new account is created (name and Telegram ID), used to monitor the service.

5. Processors and where data is stored

The app and its PostgreSQL database are hosted on Heroku (Salesforce, Inc.) in the United States region. Transfers from the EU/EEA rely on Salesforce's certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses in Heroku's data processing addendum. Telegram (Telegram FZ-LLC / Telegram Messenger Inc.) processes your identity and payments under its own privacy policy; we receive from Telegram only the fields listed in section 2. We may move hosting to an EU region; this policy will be updated if so.

6. Retention

  • Account, links, points, notifications: until you delete your account.
  • Invitation tokens: deleted with the account; unused tokens expire automatically.
  • Consent records: until you delete your account. Because a record also belongs to the other person, on deletion your ID in their copy is replaced with a "deleted user" placeholder and any active record is marked revoked.
  • Payment records: kept for accounting after account deletion, with your ID replaced by the placeholder.
  • Server logs: retained by the hosting provider for a short rolling window (typically up to 7 days).
  • Database backups: daily, kept by the hosting provider for up to 7 days, then overwritten.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can access, correct, export, restrict and delete your data, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and complain to a supervisory authority (in Portugal: CNPD, cnpd.pt). Inside the app: ⋯ menu → Settings → Privacy & data gives you Export my data (JSON) and Delete my account, both immediate and self-service. For anything else, use the contact below.

8. Age

The service is intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly process data of minors; if you believe a minor is using the app, contact us.

9. Security

Requests are authenticated with Telegram's signed initData (checked for signature and freshness); invitation links are random single-use tokens; traffic is TLS-encrypted; database credentials are rotated and not stored in code; access is rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting you, we will notify you via the bot.

10. Changes

We will post changes here with a new "last updated" date and, for material changes, notify you in the app.
